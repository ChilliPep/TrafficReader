What is the Division Operation?

The division operation is one of the operations in relational algebra. It is used when it is necessary to find values that are associated with all values from another relation.

The division operation is used when a query contains formulations such as:

  • «find X that are associated with all Y»
  • «find X for which a condition holds for every Y»
3
Database Schema

To demonstrate the division operation, the following schema of three tables is used:

Students · Courses · Enrollments

The Task
Task: "Find the names of students who are enrolled in all courses offered by the CS department."

To solve this task using relational algebra, it must be performed step by step. We determine whether a course belongs to CS by its identifier — all courses whose course_id starts with "CS" are considered CS department courses.

There are exactly two such courses: CS101 and CS102.

5
Step 1: Building the CS_Courses Relation

In the first step, we form a relation containing all courses of the CS department. This will be the divisor in the division operation.

This query selects all course identifiers that begin with "CS".

The CS department offers exactly 2 courses
6
Step 2: Building the Student_Courses Relation

In the second step, we retrieve all pairs (student_id, course_id) from the Enrollments table. This will be the dividend in the division operation.

From the table it is clear that student 101 (Alice) is enrolled in both CS courses, student 103 (Charlie) is enrolled in only one, and student 102 (Bob) is not enrolled in any CS course.

7
Step 3: Applying the Division Operation

The division operation is written as follows:

Qualified_Students = Student_Courses ÷ CS_Courses

It returns those values of student_id for which the Student_Courses relation contains pairs with all values of course_id from CS_Courses.

Step 4: Retrieving Student Names

In the final step, we join the result with the Students table to obtain student names instead of just their identifiers.
